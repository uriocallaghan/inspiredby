## sc2001 · Senghas i Coppola (2001), *Psychological Science* 12(4) — llegit sencer (PDF del laboratori)
- Història: «Before the 1970s, deaf Nicaraguans had little contact with each other» (p. 323). «one school, founded in Managua in 1977 with 25 deaf students, expanded to include 100 students in 1979 […] The following year, a vocational school opened for adolescents. By 1983, the schools served more than 400 deaf students (Polich, 1998)» (p. 324).
- «The schoolchildren all had hearing parents, and none knew any signing deaf adults. Teachers focused on teaching them to lip-read and speak Spanish, with minimal success. However, the children were allowed to communicate gesturally on the buses and school grounds.» (p. 324)
- Mètode: 24 signants; 1a cohort 1983 o abans (n = 13), 2a després (n = 11); grups d'edat <6;6, 6;6–10, >10 (n = 8 cadascun); dibuix animat de 2 minuts (p. 325).
- «signers who entered the community by age 10 produced more spatial modulations per verb (early-exposed: 1.5; middle-exposed: 1.3) than those who entered after age 10 (late-exposed: 0.7)» (p. 325).
- Referència compartida: «first cohort: 0.63; second cohort: 0.88» (p. 326); altres usos 0,48 i 0,40.
- Velocitat: «members of the second cohort signed more rapidly (234 mpm) than members of the first cohort (194 mpm)»; per edat 262, 240, 135 (p. 327).
- Experiència: «the second cohort averaged fewer years of exposure to the language (8 years) than the first cohort (14 years)» (p. 326).
- Exemple «veure» i «pagar» al mateix lloc (p. 324).
- Conclusió: «sequential cohorts of interacting young children collectively possess the capacity not only to learn, but also to create, language» (resum); «an entirely new language, one that did not exist when they were born» (p. 328); «In under two decades» (resum).
- Valors per grup del gràfic V3: llegits de les figures 2, 3b i 4 (aproximats): modulacions 1,35/1,82 · 1,12/1,70 · 0,73/0,70; referència compartida 0,80/1,33 · 0,62/1,22 · 0,36/0,41; morfemes per minut 210/350 · 208/295 · 150/128.

## sko2004 · Senghas, Kita i Özyürek (2004), *Science* 305 — versió acceptada (WRAP, Warwick)
- «Societal attitudes kept most deaf individuals at home […] In this context, no sign language emerged» (p. 3 del manuscrit). «The home sign systems developed by Nicaraguans appear to have varied widely from one deaf person to another».
- «opening of an expanded elementary school for special education in 1977, followed by a vocational school in 1981 […] approximately 50 students, growing to over 200 by 1981 […] by the mid-1980s deaf adolescents were meeting regularly on the weekends» (p. 3). Discrepància amb sc2001 (1980; 100 el 1979; >400 el 1983): es diu al peu del gràfic.
- «approximately 800 deaf signers of NSL, ranging from 4 to 45 years of age […] changes in its grammar first appear among preadolescent signers, soon spreading to subsequent, younger learners, but not to adults […] the most fluent signers are the youngest» (p. 4).
- 30 signants (10 per cohort: abans del 1984, 1984–1993, després del 1993), tots signants des dels 6 anys o abans, i 10 oients (p. 4). Dibuix: gat que s'empassa una bola de bitlles i rodola carrer avall (llegenda fig. 1).
- Proporcions: simultànies «(1.0) […] (.73) […] (.32 and .38)»; seqüencials «(0) […] (.27) […] (.78 and .73)» (p. 6).
- A-B-A: «roll descend roll […] appeared in approximately a third of the coded expressions (.37) by participants from all three cohorts […] They never appeared in the gestures of the Spanish-speakers» (p. 7–8).
- Proposta dels autors: «a dissecting, segmental approach to bundles of information […] appears to override other patterns of organization in the input» i «a predisposition for linear sequencing» (p. 9).

## coppola2020 · Coppola (2020), capítol a *Emerging Sign Languages of the Americas* — llegit sencer (còpia de l'autora)
- «ANSNIC […] was formally organized in Managua in 1986 and, with the support of the Royal Swedish Association of the Deaf, purchased a house (Polich, 2005)» (p. 439).
- «There was no previously existing deaf community or sign language in Nicaragua» (p. 440). «The sign language began to coalesce around 1978» (p. 441).
- Cohorts: «before 1983 are considered Cohort 1; those who entered between 1984 and 1993 are Cohort 2, those who entered 1994–2003 are Cohort 3» (p. 440).
- «Deaf adults often marry each other, and usually have hearing children» (p. 440–441); «transmission of the language does depend on the institutional context of education, because of the low incidence of inherited deafness» (p. 446).
- Llei 675 (2009): «Nicaraguan Sign Language is the official language of Deaf people in Nicaragua»; llei 763 (2011): «Sign language should be the language of instruction for deaf children» (taula 1, p. 446).
- «There are 25 public schools of Special Education located in the municipalities of Managua, San Marcos, Jinotepe, Diriamba, Nuevo Amanecer Community (Diriamba), Masaya, Granada, Rivas, León, La Paz Centro, Chinandega, Chichigalpa, El Viejo, Corinto, Boaco, Juigalpa, Matagalpa, Jinotega, Estelí, La Trinidad, Condega, Ocotal, Somoto, Bluefields and Bilwi» (p. 443).
- «These figures suggest that approximately 5% of deaf children in Nicaragua attend school» (p. 445); els qui viuen en zones rurals «do not have access to special education» (p. 440).
- «Judy Kegl, a linguist then based at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT), began investigating the language in 1986, made the first videorecordings in 1987, and published the first scientific report of NSL (Kegl and Iwata, 1989)» (p. 447).
- «limited contact with Swedish Sign Language; […] (after around 2010), the internet and social media facilitated contact with American Sign Language videos» (p. 442).

## polich · Polich (2005), Gallaudet University Press — fitxa de l'editorial (no s'ha llegit el llibre)
- «nine-year study […] research in Nicaragua's National Archives, field observations of deaf pupils in 20 special education schools […] a survey of 225 deaf individuals» i «Polich found that the use of a "standardized" sign language in Nicaragua did not emerge until there was a community of users meeting on a regular basis, especially beyond childhood. The adoption of NSL did not happen suddenly, but took many years and was fed by multiple influences. She also discovered the process that deaf adolescents used to attain their social agency». La mateixa fitxa diu que hi va haver «controversy […] regarding the validity of NSL as a genuinely spontaneous language created by young children». Afiliació: Speech and Hearing Sciences, Portland State University.

## ps2009 · Pyers i Senghas (2009), *Psychological Science* — text complet (PMC2884962)
- Taula 1: 1a cohort (n = 8) edat 26,8; verbs mentals 1,13 → 3,9; creença falsa 0,5 (de 4) → 1,5 (de 3). 2a cohort (n = 10) edat 17,5; 7,20 → 7,4; 3,8 → 2,6.
- «Half of the first-cohort signers produced no mental-state words at all» · «Between 2001 and 2003, newly adult second-cohort signers began socializing at the deaf association […] all of the mental-state verbs produced by first-cohort signers at Time 2 had been observed in the narratives of second-cohort signers at Time 1».

## pyers2010 · Pyers et al. (2010), *PNAS* — resum (PubMed 20616088)
- «second-cohort signers […] used more consistent spatial language than the first-cohort signers […] outperformed the first cohort in spatially guided searches, both when they were disoriented and when an array was rotated».

## kocab2016 · Kocab, Senghas i Snedeker (2016), *Cognition* — resum (PMC5027136)
- «while all three cohorts of signers could communicate about linearly ordered discrete events, only the second and third generations of signers successfully communicated information about events with more complex temporal structure».

## sandler2005 · Sandler et al. (2005), *PNAS* — text complet (PMC548320)
- «founded ≈200 years ago in the Negev […] ≈3,500 members […] ≈150 individuals with congenital deafness have been born into the community» en tres generacions; «arisen in the last 70 years»; «a significant fraction of its hearing members communicate by means of a sign language».
- «Predicate-final orders overwhelmingly outnumber non-predicate-final orders, 136 to 22»; «the local Arabic dialect's S–V–O order»; ordres diferents dels de la llengua de signes israeliana.

## winata1995 · Winata et al. (1995), *J. Med. Genet.* — resum (PubMed 7616538)
- «Bengkala […] has existed for over 700 years. Currently, 2.2% of the 2185 people in this village have profound congenital deafness […] a village specific sign language which is used by many of the hearing and deaf people».

## lutz2024 · Lutzenberger et al. (2024), *Language Learning and Development* — text complet (PMC11418908)
- «a Balinese village of ~3,000 people […] Today, 33 permanent residents are deaf […] high percentage of hearing signers […] eight babies were born into deaf households between 2014 and 2018 […] KK signs use locations that are unusual in other sign languages, e.g., the hip, the teeth, or the tongue».

## No verificat o descartat
- No s'ha trobat cap font primària per a la xifra de 1 sord de cada 5.728 nord-americans del segle XIX (comparació habitual amb Martha's Vineyard): no s'usa.
- El paper concret de Steven Pinker en la difusió del cas no s'ha verificat: no s'esmenta.

- L'any de l'escola d'oficis (1980 o 1981) i la matrícula inicial difereixen entre dues publicacions del mateix grup.

## Revisió del 20-11-2026 (tres revisors crítics)
Fonts afegides i llegides:
- **polich** (introducció, PDF de l'editorial): «there *had* been education available for deaf children since the 1940s»; mestres del període 1946–1976; ANSNIC fundada el 22-04-1986; centre de formació professional el 1981; «the story, as I had first accepted it, seriously underestimated the adolescent contribution to the formation of a deaf community and its language»; «did not emerge as an independent entity until there was a community of users meeting on a regular basis and *beyond childhood*»; «The larger Nicaraguan society only began to recognize that non-oral deaf persons could be social actors after an organized deaf association formed»; fins als anys seixanta les llengües de signes no es consideraven llengües (Stokoe 1960). No s'hi ha trobat el nom «Escuela No. 1»: no s'usa.
- **ley675** (text de la llei, PDF de Justia): art. 2 «creado por las personas con discapacidad auditiva en Nicaragua»; art. 3 «medio de comunicación oficial de las personas con discapacidad auditiva»; art. 7 dret a l'educació.
- **sko2004** material suplementari: «Five of the Spanish-speakers, and two of the first-cohort signers, never produced such expressions»; 70 expressions; tres escenes; dades agrupades.
